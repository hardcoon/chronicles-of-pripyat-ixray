Sleep Timelapse 0.31: original concept, camera easing and alarm by TheMrDemonized.
Original addon: https://www.moddb.com/mods/stalker-anomaly/addons/modded-exes-required-sleep-timelapse-01
IX-Ray adaptation for Chronicles of Pripyat: Hardcoon.

Enable or disable Sleep timelapse in GAME settings. Default: enabled.
Camera elevation is traced from the level bounds over the actual roof geometry.
Occluded departure paths use a fade; no safe point means ordinary sleep.
F5/F9 and rebound quick save/load are blocked during the sequence.
Damage interrupts sleep. Time, sound, input and ALife distance are restored.
Uses COP-ENG-185. Save-game serialization and static spawn data are unchanged.
